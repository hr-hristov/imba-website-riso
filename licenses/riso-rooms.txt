The printed rooms on this site follow the technique of riso-rooms
(github.com/IshaanKalra2103/riso-rooms): every fill knocks the paper clean and
overprints one ink as a halftone at its own screen angle, the lines are drawn
by hand from noise, each ink plate sits a hair off register, and the whole
print boils through three variants. Its notice follows.
